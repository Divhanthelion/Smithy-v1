2026-07-31 — Two ceilings and a fisherman

The agent's context ceiling was decorative 9198cdc

Both providers implemented probe_model as an inherent method, but the app calls it through Arc<dyn Provider> — and inherent methods do not dispatch through a trait object. Every call landed on the trait's Ok(None) default, every session silently fell back to the conservative 32k warn / 110k hard ceilings, and a cloud model with a 1,000,000-token window was capped like a small local one. The warning in the agent panel ("context ceiling reached") was the fallback firing, not the model.

Resumed sessions made it stickier: they replayed their stored limits, so any session created while the probe was being swallowed carried the tiny ceilings forever. Resume now re-derives ceilings from the live endpoint whenever the probe answers and keeps stored values only when it is silent — so old sessions heal on next launch.

The fisherman got looked at 3202f86

He had only ever been tuned by arithmetic. Now there is a headless renderer — examples/fisherman_preview.rs (tiny-skia, dev-dependency only) — that draws the pose lineup, the walk cycle frame by frame, and the whole rail at true size and 3× zoom: 

cargo run -p smithy-editor --example fisherman_preview
# writes /tmp/fisherman/{poses,walk,scene_1x,scene_3x}.png

What looking revealed:

  • The morning stretch was invisible. The raised arm's elbow sat inside the head circle and was overpainted by it. It now swings out and clears the crown.
  • The saunter was never drawn. The standing pose was measured for a shouldered rod, but the rod was gated to the perch — he walked with a bent arm around nothing. The rod now draws on every walk, re-seated so the shaft rests on the shoulder with his hand on it, never through the head.
  • The fire was a dim triangle. It has a hearth glow, taller licks, and rising sparks now.
  • The window showed him home before he arrived. The occupant silhouette no longer appears during the walk whose destination is the hut.
  • Lamplight spills through the door on his evening return, via door_glow(lit, openness) — a shut door or a dark room can never glow.

Four new tests pin the geometry: the stretch clears the head, the shaft rests on the shoulder with the hand on it, and the doorway only glows when there is light to spill.

Still on the bench

  • CURLED, READING_POSE and the whole waking blend are indoor-only — he is never drawn for them, only the window silhouette shows. An afternoon book on the doorstep would give that pose an audience.
  • The pose lineup and scene tiles in the preview are a copy of the app's draw logic, kept in sync by hand. If the two drift, the preview lies. A shared "scene description" layer would end the duplication, at the cost of a refactor the current size does not yet justify.
